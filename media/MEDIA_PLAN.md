# TenderLoop project media

## Brand direction

TenderLoop uses a calm editorial system rather than a children’s cartoon or clinical-health aesthetic. Warm ivory creates safety, forest green signals agency and trust, clay represents human care, and quiet blue is reserved for information and temporary access.

The mark uses two continuous arcs as a bridge between student and family. They surround—but do not expose—the protected center. It avoids humanoid AI, chat bubbles, surveillance symbolism, and parent-as-supervisor imagery.
